FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take? +
Most orders are delivered within 30–45 minutes. Delivery time depends on your location, restaurant preparation time, and traffic conditions.
What is the delivery fee? +
We charge ₹40 delivery fee for orders below ₹500. Orders above ₹500 enjoy FREE delivery. Orders above ₹600 also get an automatic 5% discount!
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept Cash on Delivery, UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), and Credit/Debit cards. Choose the most convenient option during checkout.
Can I cancel or modify my order? +
You can cancel orders within 2 minutes of placing them. Once the restaurant begins preparation, cancellations may not be possible. Contact our support team for urgent changes.
How do I become a restaurant partner? +
We'd love to have you on board! Send us an email at partners@foodflow.in or fill in the contact form above selecting "Restaurant Partnership" as the subject.
