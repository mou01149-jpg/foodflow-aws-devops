About FoodFlow

On a mission to make great food accessible to everyone, everywhere.

Our Story

Born from a Passion for Great Food

FoodFlow was founded in 2022 by a group of college students who were tired of waiting hours for mediocre delivery from unreliable apps. We wanted something better — faster, tastier, more reliable.

Starting with just 5 restaurant partners in Hyderabad, FoodFlow has grown to partner with 500+ restaurants across 200+ cities. Our platform makes it effortless to discover new restaurants, order your favourite meals, and get them delivered in record time.

We believe food is more than sustenance — it's culture, memory, and joy. Our goal is to bring restaurant-quality experiences to your home, every single day.
